# Privacy Policy

**Lexicon Ladder** · Last updated: October 11, 2026

- The app does not require an account, and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information.
- Your game progress (completed levels and stars) is stored only on your own device. Clearing the app's data or using **Reset progress** removes it.
- The app needs internet access to load its fonts. It does not sell or share personal data.
- The app may vibrate your phone for feedback.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.

**Contact:** open an [issue](../../issues) on this repository.
