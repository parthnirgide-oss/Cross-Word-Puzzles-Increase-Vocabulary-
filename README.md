# Lexicon Ladder

A crossword puzzle game that helps you build your vocabulary. Climb the ladder from everyday words to rare ones, one puzzle at a time.

**[Download the Android app (APK)](../../releases/latest)** · **[Play in your browser](./index.html)** *(see "Play online" below)*

## Features

- **18 crossword levels** across 5 tiers, from easy to expert:
  - **Apprentice**: everyday words, used with more precision
  - **Reader**: words from books and newspapers
  - **Scholar**: academic and formal vocabulary
  - **Wordsmith**: words that sharpen writing and speech
  - **Lexicographer**: rare words that mark an expert reader
- Clue-based crosswords with an on-screen keyboard
- **Check** and **Reveal word** hints when you get stuck
- Star ratings for each level
- Progress saved on your device, with a **Reset progress** option
- Light and dark mode

## Install on Android

1. Open the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on your phone.
2. Tap **Lexicon-Ladder-v1.0.0.apk** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser, then tap **Install**.
4. Open **Lexicon Ladder** and play.

Works on Android phones and tablets, including Samsung Galaxy devices.

> **Safety tip:** only install APKs from sources you trust. You can check your download matches this release with its SHA-256 checksum:
>
> `f9cece17880982de4ae052870c6a179c8607d4c67cecaec6e12fc3ba05b0ff0e`
>
> On Android you can check it with any file-hash app. On a computer run `sha256sum Lexicon-Ladder-v1.0.0.apk`.

## Play online

This repository includes `index.html`, the whole game in one file. To play it in a browser, turn on **GitHub Pages**:

1. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
2. Under **Branch**, choose `main` and the `/ (root)` folder, then **Save**.
3. After a minute, the game is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/`.

You can also just open `index.html` on any device. An internet connection is needed to load the game fonts; the game still works with fallback fonts without it.
